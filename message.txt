To my Master’s sheep, whether in the fold or the one out in the wilderness:



The book of Hebrews 4:9-11 states, “There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God. For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his. Let us labour therefore to enter into that rest, lest any man fall after the same example of unbelief.”



We at Bethlehem Primitive Baptist church gather each Sunday to draw near in full assurance of faith, trusting not in our own works of righteousness but in the finished work of Salvation by God the Father (who foreknew and drew us), God the Son (Jesus Christ who redeemed us by his precious blood), and the washing of regeneration by God the Holy Spirit. Resting in the eternal security of his Grace and Mercy, watching and waiting until the day our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ return to bring the whole and elect family of God, without the loss of one, home to Glory. Having been chosen in him before the foundation of the world, and predestinated to be conformed to his image. Proclaiming the glorious gospel of liberty and declaring the Victory over Death, Hell, and the Grave by the Resurrection of Jesus Christ. 



We’d love all those of like mind to come and worship with us!
